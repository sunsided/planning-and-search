algorithm saturated-cost-partitioning(A, order, cost)
  input: abstractions A₁, …, A_k, an order of their indices, operator costs cost
  output: goal distances h_i and a cost function sat_i for each abstraction
  remaining ← cost
  for each i in order do
    h_i ← goal-distances(A_i, remaining)
    for each operator o do
      sat_i[o] ← max({h_i[a] − h_i[b] : (a, o, b) ∈ T_i, h_i[a] ≠ ∞, h_i[b] ≠ ∞} ∪ {0})
