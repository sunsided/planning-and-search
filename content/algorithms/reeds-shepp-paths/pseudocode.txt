algorithm Reeds-Shepp-Path(p, q, ρ)
  input: start pose p, goal pose q, minimum turning radius ρ
  output: the shortest Reeds-Shepp path from p to q
  (x, y, φ) ← q in the frame of p, scaled by 1/ρ   ▷ local-goal
  best ← nil
  for each family F in CSC, CCC, CCCC, CCSC, CCSCC do
    for each variant (x′, y′, φ′, sign) in symmetric-variants(x, y, φ) do
      ▷ the identity, time flip, reflection, and both together
      if F has a solution (t, u, v) at (x′, y′, φ′) then
        lengths ← sign · layout(F, t, u, v)      ▷ a negative length drives in reverse
        if best = nil or ∑ |lengths| < ∑ |best| then
          best ← lengths
  return path of best, scaled by ρ              ▷ a CSC word always has a solution
