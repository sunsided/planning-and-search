algorithm Tabu-Search(Q, M, tenure, budget)
  input: QUBO model Q over n ≥ 1 variables, M moves, a tenure
  input: budget of moves
  output: the lowest-energy assignment visited, with its energy
  x ← n uniform random bits
  E ← energy(Q, x)
  for i in 0, …, n − 1 do
    Δ[i] ← flip-delta(Q, x, i)
  free[i] ← 0 for every i                          ▷ the first move at which i is not tabu
  best, E-best ← x, E
  t̄ ← min(tenure, n − 1)                          ▷ leaves some variable allowed
  for m in 0, …, M − 1 do
    if exhausted(budget) then
      return best, E-best                          ▷ resume continues at move m
    i ← the lowest-index i of least Δ[i] among those with free[i] ≤ m or E + Δ[i] < E-best
    E ← E + Δ[i]                                   ▷ uphill moves are taken too
    x[i] ← ¬x[i]
    update Δ[i] and Δ[j] for each neighbor j of i
    free[i] ← m + 1 + t̄
    if E < E-best then
      best, E-best ← x, E
  return best, E-best
