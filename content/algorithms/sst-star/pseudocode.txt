algorithm SST*(x₀, δ-BN, δ-s, ξ, K₀)
  input: start state x₀, initial radii δ-BN and δ-s, shrink factor ξ in (0, 1), first epoch K₀
  output: a sequence of trajectories whose cost converges to the optimum
  T ← SST tree with root x₀
  j ← 0; K ← K₀                                   ▷ epoch j lasts K iterations
  repeat
    for i in 1, …, K do
      SST-Step(T, δ-BN, δ-s)                    ▷ one iteration of SST
    yield cheapest trajectory to a goal node of T
    δ-BN ← ξ · δ-BN
    δ-s ← ξ · δ-s
    j ← j + 1
    K ← ⌊(1 + ln j) · ξ^(−(d + l + 1) j) · K₀⌋    ▷ d: state, l: control dimension
  until the iteration budget is spent
