algorithm TPA(z^I, z^N, solve)
  input: ideal point z^I, strict upper bound z^N of the nadir point, a subproblem solver
  output: the Pareto set, complete when no box is left
  BOXES ← {[z^I, z^N)}                   ▷ half-open boxes in objective space
  FRONT ← ∅
  j ← p                                  ▷ p objectives; the first pick uses direction 1
  while BOXES ≠ ∅ do
    j ← next direction after j whose queue is not empty, cyclic
    B ← box of highest priority in the queue of direction j
    remove B from BOXES
    ω_i ← 1 / max(1, B.upper_i − z^I_i) for every i
    x, z ← solve(min of max_i ω_i (z_i − z^I_i) + ϵ · ∑_i ω_i (z_i − z^I_i))
    if score(z) < 1 then                 ▷ a new Pareto point inside B
      FRONT ← FRONT ∪ {(x, z)}
      for each box A in BOXES ∪ {B} with z < A.upper do
        replace A by its p-partition at z
      join the boxes with comparable upper bounds
      set the reduced scaled priority of every new box
  return FRONT
